Trained on real CWC river discharge + rainfall records, 2001–2025, across 5 Kerala gauge stations (KUTTYADI, MUTHANKERA, PERUMANNU, THUMPAMON, VANDIPERIYAR). Flood events are rare (~1% of days), so this is deployed as a screening / early-warning signal, not a precise detector — of every high-risk alert it raises, roughly 1 in 9 corresponds to a real flood day, and it catches roughly 1 in 3 real flood events at its operating threshold (0.8). Validated against the August 2018 Kerala floods at VANDIPERIYAR (9 of 13 confirmed flood days correctly flagged) — note this check is not a clean time-based holdout, since most of those days were included in the training split.
